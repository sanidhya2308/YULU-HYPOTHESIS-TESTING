LinkedIn Post Draft: Data Analytics in Action — From Walmart to Yulu
🧠 Turning Data into Business Growth: Two Real-World Case Studies

In today’s business landscape, data analytics isn’t optional — it’s strategic.
To understand how data fuels smarter decisions, I explored two contrasting case studies: Walmart (Retail Analytics) and Yulu (Mobility Analytics).

Both reveal one truth 👇

“When businesses listen to their data, they unlock new paths for growth.”

🏬 Case Study 1: Walmart — Retail Analytics

🎯 Objective:
Understand what drives Walmart’s weekly sales across multiple stores and holidays.

🧩 Approach:

Performed EDA (Exploratory Data Analysis) to identify sales trends.

Conducted Feature Engineering to capture effects of holidays, temperature, and fuel price.

Built Regression Models to forecast sales.

📊 Key Insights:
1️⃣ Holiday Weeks Boost Sales:
Thanksgiving & Christmas drive peak revenue.
2️⃣ Temperature Impacts Sales:
Hotter regions show stronger seasonal fluctuations.
3️⃣ Store Type Matters:
Larger stores (Type A) outperform smaller formats consistently.
4️⃣ Fuel Prices Have Minor Impact:
Economic factors play a smaller short-term role.

💼 Business Implications:

Use holiday-based promotions for demand spikes.

Implement regional inventory planning based on temperature trends.

Focus marketing on high-performing store types.

🚴‍♀️ Case Study 2: Yulu — Mobility Analytics

🎯 Objective:
Identify factors influencing bike rental demand and validate through hypothesis testing.

🧩 Approach:

Performed EDA + statistical tests (T-Test, ANOVA, Chi-Square)

Compared demand across seasons, weather, and working days.

📊 Key Insights:
1️⃣ Season & Weather Drive Demand:
Rentals highest in Summer & Fall, lowest during Winter or rainy weather.
2️⃣ Temperature (15–30°C) is the sweet spot for maximum rentals.
3️⃣ Working Days: Slightly higher usage but not statistically significant.
4️⃣ Weather & Season are Dependent: Clear weather dominates in summer months.

💼 Business Implications:

Dynamic Pricing: Offer discounts during low-demand seasons.

Weather-Based Fleet Planning: Adjust availability during bad weather.

Predictive Analytics: Use models to forecast demand daily.

🧩 Common Themes Across Walmart & Yulu
Aspect	Walmart	Yulu

🧠 Learning Takeaways

📍 Both projects highlight the power of descriptive & inferential analytics.
📍 Business problems, when translated into data models, reveal hidden opportunities.
📍 Storytelling with data = bridging analytics and strategy.
